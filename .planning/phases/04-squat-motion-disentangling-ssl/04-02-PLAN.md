## Multi-rep handling (resolved at probe time per RESEARCH §1 pt.4)

The paper claims samples are single-rep (§4 p.8). split_half_cycles uses the global extremum (argmax/argmin), correct for single-rep. Task 1's probe REPORTS how many of the 4,970 trajectories have more than 1 prominent extremum (via scipy.signal.find_peaks). IF the multi-rep tail is non-trivial, Task 2 adds first-rep extraction (find_peaks prominence + take first descent+ascent); IF negligible, the global-extremum split is sufficient. This is a measured decision, not a guess.
